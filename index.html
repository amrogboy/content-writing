Questions

Before you ask.

Do I have to buy all four services?

No. Many clients start with one or two. The engine works best with all four, and we will show you where the biggest gap is before you commit.

How soon will we see results?

Paid ads can show signal in the first month. Organic and SEO usually take three to six months to build, which is why we run them next to paid from the start.

What is GEO, and do I need it?

Generative engine optimization makes your content easy for AI assistants to find, trust and cite. If your buyers ask ChatGPT or Perplexity for recommendations, yes.

Who owns the ad accounts and content?

You do. Accounts sit under your business, and all creative, footage and content are yours to keep if we part ways.
